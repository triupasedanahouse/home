The local side of Bali

Experience Bali
beyond the tourist trail.

Bali is more than busy streets and packed itineraries. Here, the village moves at its own pace—leaving room for shared meals, gentle mornings and small encounters that feel personal.

Explore what is nearby →
01

Walk the village

Take a slower walk through rice fields, bamboo paths and the everyday landscape of Kenderan.

02

Follow the subak

Learn how water, farming and community are connected through Bali’s traditional rice-field system.

03

Explore by bike

See more of the village at an easy pace and pause where the view invites you to stay awhile.

04

Visit sacred water

Ask about visiting Beji Telaga Waja, a local place connected with the Balinese water-purification tradition.

05

Find Manuaba Waterfall

Follow a green route toward a forest waterfall—an ideal stop for nature lovers and quiet explorers.

06

Odalan & dance

When an Odalan temple ceremony is taking place, guests may have the chance to respectfully observe offerings, music and traditional dance.

This is not a scheduled performance. Ceremony dates, access and visitor etiquette are always guided by the local community.
